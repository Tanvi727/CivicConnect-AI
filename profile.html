Help & Support

Frequently asked questions

How do I report a civic issue?

Open Report Issue, choose a category, describe the problem, add the location and a clear photo, then submit. You can follow it from My Reports.

How can I check the status of my report?

Open My Reports. Each report shows its current status: Submitted, Under Review, In Progress or Resolved.

Why is my mobile number or date of birth missing?

Accounts created before these fields were added may not have them. Enter them under Edit Personal Information and press Save Changes.

Is my data stored securely?

In this version your information is kept in your own browser on this device. Use Download my data to keep a copy, and avoid using a shared computer for your account.
